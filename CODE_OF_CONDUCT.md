# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be respectful. Be constructive. Focus on the work.

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Disagreement is fine. Hostility is not.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks or insults
- Harassment of any kind
- Publishing others' private information
- Trolling or deliberately inflammatory comments

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct.

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers directly.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, PRs, discussions, and any public space where you represent this project.
